Rules & Regulations

  1. Registration is free for all participants.
  2. The last date for submission of the registration form is 10th November 2026 until 5:00 PM.
  3. Final fixtures will be released on 12th November after 1:00 PM.
  4. The tournament is open to all badminton players.
  5. The tournament will be conducted on a knockout basis.
  6. Up to the Quarter Finals: Best of three sets (15 points per set).
    Semi Finals and Finals: Best of three sets (21 points per set).
  7. BWF & BAI rules will be followed.
  8. A player can participate in a maximum of two events only.
  9. Players participating in age-group events must produce a birth certificate or Aadhaar card.
  10. The combined age of veteran players shall not be less than 85 years, and both players must be 35 years or above.
  11. The combined age of the players in a Mixed Veteran Doubles pair shall not be less than 80 years, and one of the players may be 30 years or above.
  12. The matches shall be played using Yonex AS-2 feather shuttlecocks.
  13. Reporting time for the Junior category is 8:00 AM and for the Senior category is 2:00 PM on 13 November 2026.
  14. Cash prizes:
    • Age Categories: Winner – ₹3,000/- | Runner-up – ₹1,500/-
    • Open and Veteran Categories: Winner – ₹15,000/- | Runner-up – ₹8,000/-
  15. The decision of the organising committee and match officials will be final and binding.
  16. The participants shall not receive any travelling allowance, food, or lodging from the organisers.

Registration Closed
